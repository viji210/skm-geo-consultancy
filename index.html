FAQ

Common Questions
Answered

What is a soil investigation and why do I need one?

+
A soil investigation (geotechnical investigation) characterizes the subsurface soil and rock conditions at your construction site. It is essential for designing safe and economic foundations — without it, engineers must make conservative assumptions that often result in over-designed (costly) or under-designed (unsafe) foundations.

How long does a soil investigation typically take?

+
Field investigation typically takes 1–5 days depending on the number of boreholes and site size. Laboratory testing and report preparation usually require 7–14 additional working days. We provide estimated timelines upfront after the initial consultation.

What types of projects do you serve?

+
We serve residential buildings, villas, apartments, industrial factories, manufacturing plants, bridges, highways, and infrastructure projects of all scales — from single-floor homes to large government and commercial projects across Tamil Nadu.

Do you follow Indian Standards (IS codes)?

+
Yes. All our investigations and reports comply with relevant BIS standards including IS 1892, IS 2131, IS 2720, and IS 6403, as well as internationally recognized ASTM and AASHTO standards where applicable.

What is included in your geotechnical report?

+
Our reports include borehole logs, soil classification, laboratory test results (grain size, Atterberg limits, compaction, shear strength, consolidation), bearing capacity analysis, settlement estimates, groundwater level, and specific foundation recommendations with allowable loads.

How do I get started with SKM Geo Consultancy?

+
Simply contact us via phone, email, or the inquiry form on this page. Share the basic project details (location, structure type, area) and we will schedule a free preliminary consultation to discuss your requirements and provide a cost estimate.
